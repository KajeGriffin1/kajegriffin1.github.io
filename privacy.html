Privacy Policy

We collect personal information from you, including information about your: name, contact information, interactions with us, and billing or purchase information.

We collect your personal information in order to: provide dog grooming services, including booking, billing, and animal care.

Besides our staff, we share this information with: your veterinarian, if requested in order to ensure your dogs medical requirements are being met.

Providing some information is optional. If you choose not to enter contact or billing information, we'll be unable to provide grooming services.. We keep your information safe by storing it in encrypted files and ensuring authorised access only . We keep your information for one year, after their last grooming appointment, at which point we securely destroy it by securely erasing all digital data.. You have the right to ask for a copy of any personal information we hold about you, and to ask for it to be corrected if you think it is wrong. If you’d like to ask for a copy of your information, or to have it corrected, please contact us at snazzypaws.biz.nz.

If you have any questions regarding this statement or the way we collect and use your information, please contact us via the contact form.
